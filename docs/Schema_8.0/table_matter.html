KR_DatabaseSchema/Clients and Matters/matter
Table definition · Clients and Matters

matter

The firm matter (case file): matter number, title, practice area, status, responsible users, cover image, and lifecycle dates. It is the central hub that time, billing, documents, tasks, events, and communications attach to.

Domain: Clients and Matters

    originating_attorney_user_id TEXT,

    FOREIGN KEY (practice_area_lookup_id)     REFERENCES lookup(id),
