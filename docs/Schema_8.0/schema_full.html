Identity and Parties

Natural-person identity records and the links between them. Every human in the system (client, user, employee, trustee, beneficiary) starts as an individual.

  • individual — The master record for a real human being.
  • individual_relationship — A relationship between two individuals (spouse, parent, child, guardian, ward, sibling, emergency contact) with the relationship named in both directions, a current/former status, and validity dates.
  • contact_resolution — An audit and idempotency record for contact clean-up operations (merge, copy-and-inactivate, inactivate).

Contacts, Addresses, and Communication Methods

Reusable addresses and contact methods for individuals and entities, kept as dated history rather than overwritten columns.

  • address — A reusable physical or mailing address.
  • individual_address — Dated address assignment for an individual: which address, what type (home, mailing, work), whether it is the primary one, and when it was valid.
  • individual_contact_method — An email, phone, fax, web address, or other contact method for an individual, with context, primary flag, verification state, texting permission, and do-not-contact controls.
  • entity_address — Dated address assignment for an entity: business, mailing, billing, registered-office, or other address context with validity dates and a primary flag.
  • entity_contact — Links an entity to an individual who serves in a role for it (trustee, officer, manager, registered agent, authorized representative, related contact).
  • entity_contact_method — An email, phone, fax, web address, or other contact method for an entity, with context, primary flag, verification state, and do-not-contact controls.

Clients and Matters

The client role, joint-client members, matters (case files), matter parties, and the matter stage history.

  • client — The client (or prospect) role attached to exactly one individual or one entity.
  • client_member — Additional individuals or entities who belong to a client record: joint clients, spouses, co-clients, authorized contacts, and billing contacts.
  • matter — The firm matter (case file): matter number, title, practice area, status, responsible users, cover image, and lifecycle dates.
  • matter_party — Links a matter to a client, individual, or entity with a party role, billing responsibility, and signature flags.
  • matter_stage_history — Ledger of matter status/stage transitions with timestamps, acting user, reason, and prior/new values.

Intake and Lead Management

Lead capture, pipeline stages, sources, and conversion from lead to client.

Notes, Communications, Tasks, and Reminders

Day-to-day work records: notes, the communication log, the workstream activity feed, tasks, and reminders.

  • kr_note — Durable internal note attached to a matter, client, individual, entity, task, event, or other owner.
  • communication_log — Normalized record of calls, emails, meetings, text messages, and other communications for a matter, client, lead, or contact.
  • communication_participant — Participants in a communication-log entry: users, clients, individuals, entities, or free-form external parties.
  • communication_attachment — Attachment link for a communication-log entry, pointing to a media asset, document record, external URL, or descriptive reference.
  • workstream_activity — The workstream feed: dated, internal-only activity entries (phone calls, emails, notes, tasks, received and sent files, work in progress) recorded against exactly one client or one lead, optionally tied to a matter, document, and task.
  • workstream_activity_comment — A user comment on a workstream activity entry.
  • matter_activity_log — Matter activity-history rail: dated work history with project identifier, task-assignment snapshot, user linkage, and narrative details.
  • task — An assignable work item with optional matter or client link, status, priority, assignee, assigner, lead user, due date, completion date, and billing-readiness fields (value, status, description).
  • task_assignee — Assignment of users to a task as assignee or collaborator, with who assigned them, when, and whether the assignment is current.
  • reminder — Operational reminder for a user or owner record, with due date, snooze/dismiss lifecycle, priority, and owner context.

  • billing_activity_code — Dictionary of billing and time-entry activity codes, with descriptions, default billing modes, and optional default rates.

Billing, Ledger, Payments, and Trust

Matter billing setup, invoices, the receivable/trust ledger, payments (including online payment requests), bank deposits, and trust requests.

  • matter_billing_profile — Matter-level billing setup: fee arrangement, billing client, receivable client, billing category, reporting group, rates, flat-fee amount, and invoice visibility defaults.
  • matter_fee_component — A flat-fee, retainer, hourly-cap, or expense-allowance component attached to a matter billing profile.
  • matter_balance_snapshot — Point-in-time matter financial summary imported from an external practice-management system: work in progress, receivables, trust balance, retainer/credits, expense buckets, staff, billing category, and reporting group.
  • invoice — Billing invoice header for a matter and billed client: invoice number, dates, totals, status, and notes.
  • invoice_line_item — Detail lines on an invoice: hourly time, flat fees, expenses, credits, discounts, taxes, adjustments, and note-only rows.
  • ledger_entry — The financial ledger: payments, credits, debits, write-offs, refunds, and adjustments applied to an invoice, matter, and client, each in a ledger bucket (for example receivable or trust) with a direction and amount.
  • ledger_audit_log — Audit trail for ledger-entry changes: actor, time, action, and JSON before/after snapshots.
  • payment — A payment or receipt header that can be applied to one or more invoices or matters and optionally grouped into a bank deposit.
  • payment_application — Application of part or all of a payment to a specific invoice, matter, and ledger entry.
  • payment_request — A client-facing online payment link for an invoice.
  • payment_provider — Configuration row per payment provider and environment (live or sandbox).
  • bank_deposit — Bank deposit slip header: deposit number and date, memo, destination bank account, totals, teller/depositor fields, and a source-document link.
  • bank_deposit_item — One line on a deposit slip (online payment, cash, check, ACH, wire, other) with matter, receipt number, drawer, masked account data, and amount.
  • firm_bank_account — Firm bank account metadata (operating, trust, merchant, other).
  • trust_request — A trust or retainer request for a matter and client: requested amount, due date, status, payment context, and the ledger entry that satisfied it.

System Metadata and Configuration

Controlled vocabularies, custom fields, bootstrap state, the maintenance gate, and diagnostics.

  • lookup — Controlled vocabulary values (category, value, label) used by statuses, workflow labels, priorities, categories, relationship types, and other reusable UI lists.
  • custom_field_set — A named group of custom field definitions for a target record type, so modules can add metadata without altering core tables.
  • custom_field_definition — Definition of one custom field: label, data type, validation, display behavior, and ordering within its set.
  • custom_field_value — Stored value of a custom field for a target row, split into text, number, date, boolean, and JSON columns.
  • kr_homebase_initialization — Single-purpose bootstrap record showing whether KR Homebase has been initialized and by which first administrator.
  • kr_maintenance_gate — Single-row switch (id is always krhomebase) that puts the application into maintenance mode, with the reason and who set it.
  • kr_crud_worker_smoke_test — Scratch table used to smoke-test the CRUD worker (create, read, update, delete) against a database.

View invoice_ledger_summary

CREATE VIEW invoice_ledger_summary AS
SELECT
    i.id AS invoice_id,
    i.matter_id,
    i.billed_to_client_id,
    i.invoice_number,
    i.total_amount,
    COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN le.voided_at IS NULL AND le.ledger_bucket = 'receivable' AND le.entry_direction = 'credit' THEN le.amount ELSE 0 END), 0) AS total_credits,
    COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN le.voided_at IS NULL AND le.ledger_bucket = 'receivable' AND le.entry_direction = 'debit' THEN le.amount ELSE 0 END), 0) AS total_debits,
    i.total_amount
      - COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN le.voided_at IS NULL AND le.ledger_bucket = 'receivable' AND le.entry_direction = 'credit' THEN le.amount ELSE 0 END), 0)
      + COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN le.voided_at IS NULL AND le.ledger_bucket = 'receivable' AND le.entry_direction = 'debit' THEN le.amount ELSE 0 END), 0) AS balance_due
FROM invoice i
LEFT JOIN ledger_entry le ON le.invoice_id = i.id
GROUP BY i.id

View matter_ledger_bucket_summary

CREATE VIEW matter_ledger_bucket_summary AS
SELECT
    matter_id,
    client_id,
    ledger_bucket,
    COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN voided_at IS NULL AND entry_direction = 'debit' THEN amount ELSE 0 END), 0) AS total_debits,
    COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN voided_at IS NULL AND entry_direction = 'credit' THEN amount ELSE 0 END), 0) AS total_credits,
    COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN voided_at IS NULL AND entry_direction = 'debit' THEN amount ELSE 0 END), 0)
      - COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN voided_at IS NULL AND entry_direction = 'credit' THEN amount ELSE 0 END), 0) AS net_debit_balance
FROM ledger_entry
GROUP BY matter_id, client_id, ledger_bucket

Triggers

Trigger public_content_sequence_after_insert on public_content

CREATE TRIGGER public_content_sequence_after_insert
AFTER INSERT ON public_content
WHEN NEW.content_type IN ('article', 'answer')
  AND (NEW.display_sequence IS NULL OR NEW.display_code IS NULL)
BEGIN
  INSERT OR IGNORE INTO content_sequence (content_type, next_sequence, updated_at)
  VALUES (NEW.content_type, 1, datetime('now'));

  UPDATE public_content
  SET
    display_sequence = COALESCE(
      NEW.display_sequence,
      (SELECT next_sequence FROM content_sequence WHERE content_type = NEW.content_type)
    ),
    display_code = COALESCE(
      NEW.display_code,
      CASE NEW.content_type
        WHEN 'answer' THEN 'Answer ' || printf('%03d', COALESCE(
          NEW.display_sequence,
          (SELECT next_sequence FROM content_sequence WHERE content_type = NEW.content_type)
        ))
        ELSE 'Article ' || printf('%03d', COALESCE(
          NEW.display_sequence,
          (SELECT next_sequence FROM content_sequence WHERE content_type = NEW.content_type)
        ))
      END
    )
  WHERE id = NEW.id;

  UPDATE content_sequence
  SET
    next_sequence = CASE
      WHEN next_sequence <= (SELECT display_sequence FROM public_content WHERE id = NEW.id)
        THEN (SELECT display_sequence FROM public_content WHERE id = NEW.id) + 1
      ELSE next_sequence
    END,
    updated_at = datetime('now')
  WHERE content_type = NEW.content_type;
END
